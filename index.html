Method

How sites are chosen

  1. Model. Each feature's CD is a Bayesian Bossung surface in log-dose and focus (14-term polynomial, normal-inverse-gamma prior), refit after every batch.
  2. Window. Every posterior draw gives a full focus-exposure window; DOF at the required EL is read off each draw, so the interval is the spread over draws.
  3. Next sites. A site is worth measuring if its three CD readings would move the DOF estimate: score = Σk Cov(DOF, CDk)² / (Var CDk + σ²). Batches are picked greedily with a rank-one covariance update between picks.
  4. Stop. Measure until the 90% interval on DOF is within ±5 nm.
